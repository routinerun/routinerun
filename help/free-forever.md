# What stays free, forever

Everything the app does is in Free. What Free limits is how many routines you keep: 3. Past that is Pro.

## What Free holds

- **3 routines**, with every feature on them: steps that do things themselves, triggers, check-ins, deadlines in your calendar or Reminders, the Lock Screen, the Dynamic Island, the Apple Watch, Siri and Shortcuts, iCloud sync, and the record of every run.
- **Runs without a count.** Start a routine as often as you like. A run is not a routine and is never counted.
- **Your history, all of it.** Nothing about a run is hidden or deleted because of a plan.

## If you were in the beta

Routine Run came out on the App Store on 25 October 2026, and the limit came with it. A build from the TestFlight beta still has no limit, and anything bought there is free and does not carry over to the App Store.

## If you pass 3 later

Nothing you already have stops. See [If Pro lapses](if-pro-lapses.md): the same rule covers somebody who never had Pro.
