# iCloud sync

Routine Run can keep your routines, runs and entries in your own iCloud, so an iPhone and an iPad signed in to it hold the same library. It is off until you turn it on. Nothing goes to a server of ours, and there is no account to make.

## Turning it on

In the app: **More › iCloud and storage › iCloud**, then **Use iCloud**. Do the same on each device.

The first time, a copy of everything on the device is kept on it, and what is already in iCloud is joined with what is here. Nothing is replaced.

On a new device, the first screen offers **Already use it on another device? Bring it from iCloud**. That brings your library instead of starting with new routines.

## What travels

- Routines, their steps and versions.
- Runs, going and finished, with their notes, values and history.
- Entries, forms, tags, places, statuses, triggers and reactions.
- Wordings you changed, and your preferences, such as hints and how lists are sorted.
- Photos in notes, by their name in iCloud Photos, not the picture itself.

**What stays on each device:** shortcut links and icons, events written to your calendar, alarms, where you are and your Focus, runs somebody sent you, and backups.

The watch gets everything from its iPhone, not from iCloud.

## Each thing happens once

Under **This device** on the same screen:

- **Cards and alarms at a routine's moments**
- **Writes to your calendar**
- **Notices where you are, your Focus and workouts**

Keep these on for one device, usually the phone. Otherwise every device puts up the same card at seven. An iPad starts with all three off.

A run you move on any device moves on all of them. Its timers and planned starts are then kept by the device that moved it last.

## What the screen says

- **Up to date**, with when.
- **Sending 3 changes:** waiting to go.
- **Changes that did not win:** two devices changed the same thing. The later change stands and the other is kept here.

Changes usually arrive by themselves. The app also asks iCloud every time you open it, so opening it is the way to hurry one along.

## A routine shared with people

From version 1.5, **Share with People…** in a routine's ••• shares that one routine with the people who run it with you, through iCloud, the way a note is shared in Notes. Sharing takes Pro on your side only, and Pro shares a routine with up to five people besides you; anybody past them gets a copy, with **Send a Copy…** in the routine's •••, which is theirs to run. An invitation nobody has used yet counts until it is; with five places taken, **Invite…** in **People** offers the copy instead. Whoever you invite joins, runs it and comments for free, and it is not one of their three unless you make them an **editor**: changing a routine is writing it, which is what the three count.

- **Who is in it** is the row under the routine's name. You choose what each may do there: an **editor** changes the routine with you, a **reviewer** runs it and signs steps off, a **runner** runs it. Everybody can comment.
- **Each person runs it on their own phone.** Their runs, notes and values stay on their phone, and yours on yours, unless somebody shows or shares a run (below).
- **Comments** have a tab of their own, in teal, on the routine's page, a run's and a step's. A comment waiting on you wears the orange dot. **Keep as a Note**, in a comment's ••• or on a long press, makes it one of your notes, with who said it: on your run or its step where it was about one, on the routine if you may change it, and otherwise under **Written down** in In hand. From there it can become a step like any note. Whoever wrote a comment can **Take Back…** it, and the routine's owner can **Take Down…** anybody's. Under each comment, 👍 ❤️ ✅ ❓ 👀 answer it with a tap instead of a sentence: a reaction puts nothing on anybody's Lock Screen, and a second tap takes yours off.
- **Show to the People in It…**, in a run's •••, lets the others see where your run of a shared routine stands: which step it is on and since when, by the routine's names for the steps, not its notes, its values or what you renamed for today. They comment on it, react, and **Suggest Next**, which reaches you as a question you answer by pressing Next or not. The run says who can see it, and **Stop Showing** ends it; it stays to be seen twelve hours after the run ends. Whoever watches is told when your run starts and when it ends, and nothing in between.
- **Share with People…**, in a run's •••, gives the run itself to everybody the routine is shared with: its steps, its name, its values, its notes and its history. If the routine is not shared yet, it is shared first, and **People** opens to invite them. The row under the run's name says who has it and opens **People**, where you choose what each may do in this run: a **runner** walks it with you, pressing Next, putting steps off and writing values and notes, and the step's actions run on the phone it was pressed on; an **editor** also changes its steps and settings; everybody else is a **reviewer**, who reads it, comments, presses **Suggest Next** and signs a step off. Their row in Runs starts with your name. **Stop Sharing This Run** makes it yours alone again and takes it off their phones.
- **Hand Over a Run…**, in a routine's •••, gives one run of it to one person: they get the run and a copy of the routine as it is now, with its steps, notes and values, and nothing else of yours: not the routine itself, its comments, your other runs or the people it is shared with. It starts a run of a copy of the routine made for that run alone, and opens it with **Invite…**: send the link to whoever is to do it. They open it, the run opens on their phone, and they walk it while you see it move on yours. **Stop Sharing This Run** takes the run and the copy off their phone. A run you started with **Run something new…** offers **Hand Over…** in its own •••. Pro on your side, iCloud on both.
- **Who Sees the Runs**, on a routine's **People** page, is the owner's one rule for every run of it: **Only Whoever Runs It** (each run stays with its runner unless they show it), **Where Each Run Is** (shown as above, from its start), **Whole Runs** (shared as above, with everybody else reading it) or **Whole Runs, Walked Together** (shared, and everybody in it can press Next). Your own runs are seen as the rule says from the next one you start. Each of the others is asked once, on their own phone, with who is in it: on the routine's page once they have joined, or on their first run of it. Whoever joins later sees them too. **Show** or **Walk Together** applies it to every run they start from then on, a run a time or a trigger starts included; **Not Now** keeps theirs theirs. They are asked again only if the owner widens the rule, and **Show My Runs** on the same page changes their answer. Runs already going stay as they are. A phone that said **Show Every Run** before version 1.6 keeps showing every run until **Show My Runs** is turned off, or **Stop Showing My Runs** in the routine's ••• is pressed. **Only the Owner and Reviewers**, under the same rule, keeps the runs to you, the editors and the reviewers: each person's runs, and what is said about them, are kept in their own iCloud and shared with you by name, so a class does not see each other's runs. Only runs started after you choose it, and only on phones with version 1.6 or later. Somebody you make an editor or reviewer later sees them too. Hand a routine to somebody who helps you, choose Walked Together, and you follow their run and move it on from your phone.
- **New People Join As**, on the same page, is what somebody you invite will be once they join, so you do not wait for them to accept before choosing. **Invite…** on that page makes a link for one person, to send in Messages, Mail or anything else; whoever opens it first joins. An invitation nobody has used yet has a row of its own, to send again or take back with a swipe, and a person is taken off the same way. Somebody who asks to join from a link they were sent waits there for **Let In**.
- **A step can wait for a sign-off.** Choose **A reviewer signs it off** under what the step waits for. The run holds there until the owner, an editor or a reviewer presses **Sign Off** on the run as it is shown to them; the run's page offers to show it while it waits.
- **A runner** sees the routine and its notes but changes nothing in it, so a run of theirs offers **Only here** for what it collected and no **Keep**.
- **Stop Sharing…** is at the foot of **People**. It takes the routine off everybody else's phone and leaves it on yours. Their runs of it stay theirs, and so does a copy somebody kept. A run one of them shared stays in your iCloud and on your phone.
- **A webhook in a routine or a run somebody shares with you** waits for your OK before your phone sends anything. The orange row under the routine's name, or the run's, lists what waits: the address, what it sends and the step it is on. **Allow** is for your phone only. It never sends a key from your keychain or anything else only your phone has, unless you tell your phone the webhook is your own. If they change the request, your phone asks again, and so does a copy you keep. The same goes for an event, a reminder, an alarm, a Home scene or a line in Health that their steps, triggers or reactions create. So does one of your shortcuts they name, with what it would be handed. What does not ask first is a reminder: a step, a time or a trigger they set can still remind you by a card, a calendar event, a reminder or an alarm.
- **Archive** on a routine somebody shares with you puts it away on your phone only. It stays as it is for everybody else, and **Take out** brings it back.
- **Leave…** takes a routine somebody shares with you off your phone. **Keep a Copy** first, if you want one of your own.

Joining one turns iCloud on for your library, and the app asks first. Nothing of it goes through us: it is in the iCloud of whoever shares the routine, and an assistant somebody else connected through our relay never reads your shared run. The one exception is a comment an assistant you connected writes for you, which passes through our relay on its way to your phone and is not kept.

## When it goes wrong

**Not signed in to iCloud.** Everything stays on this device until you sign in, in the Settings app.

**iCloud is full.** Changes wait on this device and go when there is room.

**A different iCloud account is signed in.** Nothing is sent to it until you say so.

**Something was deleted everywhere.** A delete travels like any other change. Deleted routines and runs wait in [Recently Deleted](recently-deleted.md) for 30 days.

iCloud is not a backup. **More › iCloud and storage › Back up everything** is. See [Export](export.md).

**Delete from iCloud…** takes this app's data out of iCloud. Every device keeps what it has. It also stops every routine you share with people.
