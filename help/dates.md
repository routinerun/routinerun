# How do I count things from a trip, so moving it moves everything?

Make the trip a date: **More → Dates → New date…**, name it **Departure**, and pick the day. Then count things from it rather than typing their dates: packing three days before, the car the day before, a step done by seven the evening before. Change the date, and everything counted from it moves, cards and alarms included.

## What can be counted from a date

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| A routine starting | **More → Triggers → +**, **Before or after a date**: the date, how many days before or after, and a time of day if you want one. |
| A step's time | The step's **Settings → By a time**, **Counted on → Before or after a date**: the date, the day, and the time on it. |
| A run coming back | **Not now… → Come back to it… → Before or after**, then pick the date under **From**. A date with no hour on it lands at 9:00 that day, and **At** changes it. |
| A planned run | **Plan a run**, then **Before or after** and the date. |

Each of those lists your dates, and **New date…** makes one without leaving the page.

## When the day is not known yet

Choose **Not known yet** under the name and count things from the date all the same. Nothing counted from it has a moment until then: no card, no deadline, no planned start, and the run's page says it waits for the day. When you set the day, all of it is armed at once. Anything the day puts in the past is not done late by itself: In hand asks under **A date moved**, and a planned run that is already due starts.

So that the day is not forgotten, the date asks for it, the way a check-in does. Every week at 9:00 unless you say otherwise, and only while anything is still counted from it, **In hand** lists it under **No day yet**, and the count on In hand's mark includes it. The row has **Set the day…** and **Not yet**, which asks again a whole interval later, and its **⋯** changes how often, stops it asking, or archives the date. Under the rows, **Dates with no day** opens every one of them, asking or not, so a day you learn before it asks can be set at once.

On the date, **Ask for the day** turns this off, and **Asks** takes any rhythm a check-in can: so many minutes, hours or days, the first two only between some hours if you like. **A card as well** puts it on the Lock Screen at the same moments. Setting the day is the answer: the question stops by itself.

A run's page that waits for the day says so with **Set the day…** beside it, which opens the date.

A run can wait on a date of its own too: a step's time counted from a value such as **Appointment** that the run has not said. **No day yet** lists those as well, by run, with **Set it…**, which says the value for that run alone. They are not counted on the mark.

## A moment that happens

Some moments cannot be looked up, only noticed: back from the walk, out of the door, off the train. Choose **Happens** under the name. The date then has no day to set and never asks for one. Each time the moment happens, the date becomes that moment, and everything counted after it, such as **20 minutes after**, comes for that time. If it happens twice a day, it comes twice. Nothing can be counted before it, so a moment that happens offers **When it happens** and **After** only.

What marks it is listed on the date under **What marks it**, and **Add what marks it…** makes one:

- arriving at or leaving one of your places
- a Focus coming on or ending
- a workout ending
- any other moment a trigger can wait for

Each is a trigger kept under **Triggers**, with the action **Mark a date**. A step of a routine can carry the same action, so reaching **Home** on the walk marks it.

Anything else that happens can mark it through Shortcuts: make an automation for an NFC tag by the door, CarPlay, a Wi-Fi network or an alarm, give it the action **Mark a Date**, and set it to run without asking. **Add a Date** makes one from Shortcuts or Siri.

**It happened now** on the date, **Now** on its row in **In hand** under **Moments**, or a swipe on **Dates** marks it by hand. **New moment that happens…** in In hand makes one.

If the phone hears of it late, for example a place reached with no helper shortcut and the app opened an hour later, it is marked at the time it really happened. What was counted after it and has already gone by is asked about in In hand rather than done late.

## On Apple Watch

The moment often comes with the phone in a bag or at home. On the watch, **In hand** lists your moments under **Moments** and the dates still without a day under **No day yet**. Swipe a moment for **Now**, or open it for **Now**, **10 minutes ago**, or a time ahead, such as **In 40 min** when you know you will be back in forty minutes. Open a date with no day to set it to **Now**, a time ahead, or **Other…**, chosen with the crown.

The **Moment** complication puts one moment on the watch face and opens it with one tap. Siri and Shortcuts on the watch have **Mark a Date** and **Add a Date** too.

The time is the time you pressed, not the time the phone heard. If the phone is out of reach, the press waits on the watch and reaches the phone when they are back in touch. Whatever was counted after the moment and has already gone by is asked about in In hand rather than done late.

## When a date stops mattering

Archive it: swipe it on **Dates**, press **Archive** at the foot of the date, or use the row's **⋯** in In hand. An archived date asks nothing, leaves In hand and leaves the list on **Dates**; **Show Archived** in the filter brings the archived ones back into view. Whatever is counted from it keeps waiting, and **Take out** brings it back as it was.

## Moving the date

Open the date under **Dates** and change the day. Under it, **Counted from it** lists what will move: each routine, run and trigger counted from the date, and a tap opens it. When you press **Done**, the page says how many things moved.

To put a date off, press **A day later**, **A week later**, **A month later** or **A year later** under its day, or hold it on **Dates** and pick one under **Put off**. It keeps its time of day, and everything counted from it moves too. A date that keeps coming round, such as a service every six months, is put off this way each time it has been. A date linked to an event moves with the event instead.

The search field at the top of **Dates** finds one by name. The filter beside it narrows the list to the ones with **No day yet**, **With a day** or that **Happens**, and by when they fall: gone by, in the next 7 or 30 days, or further off.

A trigger comes once for each date. If it has already come and you then move the date, it does not come again. If the move puts it in the past before it came, it does not start late by itself: **In hand** shows it under **A date moved**, with **Start** and a swipe to **Skip**.

## A run that is already going

A run counted from the date moves with it: its steps' times, a step put off and the moment it comes back. A time that had already passed and that the move puts ahead again is owed again, alarm and all.

If the run was started from the date — planned from it, or started by a trigger counted from it — and the date then slips later, the run began before it would begin now. In hand asks under **A date moved**: **Until then** puts the run down until its new start, with everything it has gathered, and it comes back on its own. Swipe for **Keep going** to leave it as it is. Nothing restarts. If the slip is small enough that the new start has already passed too, nothing is asked.

## Linking a date to your calendar

On the date, **Link to an event…** or **Link to a reminder…** picks one item. For an event, choose whether it counts from **Its start** or **Its end**. Each time you open the app, it reads that one item again, so moving the trip in Calendar moves the date here. If the event is deleted, the date keeps its last day and says so in orange.

Linking needs full access to Calendar or Reminders. Nothing fires from the event itself: it is only where the date comes from.

## When steps are already late

**In hand → Time passed** has **Move all…** for a run with several late steps. **Count them from a date…** keeps each step's time, now counted from the date you pick. From then on, moving that date moves all of them. See [Not now and Pause](pause-later-last.md).

## Sharing and assistants

A routine counted from a date takes the date with it when you share or export it. On the other phone, a date with the same name is used as it is, and a new one is made otherwise. An assistant can write these too: it names the date once with its day, and counts steps and starts from it.
