# How do I count things from a trip, so moving it moves everything?

Make the trip a date: **More → Dates → New date…**, name it **Departure**, and pick the day. Then count things from it rather than typing their dates: packing three days before, the car the day before, a step done by seven the evening before. Change the date, and everything counted from it moves, cards and alarms included.

## What can be counted from a date

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| A routine starting | **More → Triggers → +**, **Before or after a date**: the date, how many days before or after, and a time of day if you want one. |
| A step's time | The step's **Settings → By a time**, **Counted on → Before or after a date**: the date, the day, and the time on it. |
| A run coming back | **Not now… → Come back to it… → Before or after**, then pick the date under **From**. A date with no hour on it lands at 9:00 that day, and **At** changes it. |
| A planned run | **Plan a run**, then **Before or after** and the date. |

Each of those lists your dates, and **New date…** makes one without leaving the page.

## When the day is not known yet

Turn on **Day not known yet** and count things from the date all the same. Nothing counted from it has a moment until then: no card, no deadline, no planned start, and the run's page says it waits for the day. When you set the day, all of it is armed at once. Anything the day puts in the past is not done late by itself: In hand asks under **A date moved**, and a planned run that is already due starts.

## Moving the date

Open the date under **Dates** and change the day. Under it, **Counted from it** lists what will move. When you press **Done**, the page says how many things moved.

A trigger comes once for each date. If it has already come and you then move the date, it does not come again. If the move puts it in the past before it came, it does not start late by itself: **In hand** shows it under **A date moved**, with **Start** and a swipe to **Skip**.

## A run that is already going

A run counted from the date moves with it: its steps' times, a step put off and the moment it comes back. A time that had already passed and that the move puts ahead again is owed again, alarm and all.

If the run was started from the date — planned from it, or started by a trigger counted from it — and the date then slips later, the run began before it would begin now. In hand asks under **A date moved**: **Until then** puts the run down until its new start, with everything it has gathered, and it comes back on its own. Swipe for **Keep going** to leave it as it is. Nothing restarts. If the slip is small enough that the new start has already passed too, nothing is asked.

## Linking a date to your calendar

On the date, **Link to an event…** or **Link to a reminder…** picks one item. For an event, choose whether it counts from **Its start** or **Its end**. Each time you open the app, it reads that one item again, so moving the trip in Calendar moves the date here. If the event is deleted, the date keeps its last day and says so in orange.

Linking needs full access to Calendar or Reminders. Nothing fires from the event itself: it is only where the date comes from.

## When steps are already late

**In hand → Time passed** has **Move all…** for a run with several late steps. **Count them from a date…** keeps each step's time, now counted from the date you pick. From then on, moving that date moves all of them. See [Not now and Pause](pause-later-last.md).

## Sharing and assistants

A routine counted from a date takes the date with it when you share or export it. On the other phone, a date with the same name is used as it is, and a new one is made otherwise. An assistant can write these too: it names the date once with its day, and counts steps and starts from it.
